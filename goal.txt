MUC TIEU: Mo trinh duyet Chrome, vao YouTube, tim mot bai nhac bat ky.

BUOC0 - NEU MAN HINH DANG FULLSCREEN (khong thay thanh dia chi / thanh tab / thanh tim kiem):
- Neu Chrome chua foreground: [{"action":"window_activate","target":"Google Chrome"}] truoc.
- Roi nhan ESC: [{"action":"press","key":"ESC"}]  (van khong duoc thi [{"action":"press","key":"F11"}])
- Chup lai. Chi khi thay duoc thanh dia chi/thanh tim kiem moi tiep tuc.
- TUYET DOI KHONG double-click vao video/noi dung (no se bat fullscreen lai).

BUOC1 - MO/CHUYEN SANG CUA SO CHROME:
- Neu Chrome CHUA phai foreground (doc dong "Foreground" o tren): dua len truoc:
  [{"action":"window_activate","target":"Google Chrome"}]
- Neu khong tim thay, dung [{"action":"window_list"}] roi chon target {"process":"chrome.exe"}.
- Cho chu ky sau chup anh. KHONG click toa do khi chua thay ro cua so Chrome.

BUOC2 - VAO YOUTUBE:
- Mo trang YouTube:
  [{"action":"hotkey","keys":["CTRL","L"]},{"action":"type","text":"youtube.com"},{"action":"press","key":"ENTER"}]
- Cho load: [{"action":"wait","ms":4000}] roi chup lai de xem da len YouTube chua.

BUOC3 - TIM NHAC:
- BAT BUOC: click vao o tim kiem cua YouTube TRUOC, roi moi go chu. Khong duoc go mu.
- Neu o da co chu cu: click vao o -> [{"action":"hotkey","keys":["CTRL","A"]}] -> go ten bai -> ENTER.
- Ten bai nhac bat ky, vi du "lofi chill", "nhac tre hay nhat".
- TUYET DOI KHONG scroll trang de tim nhac. Phai dung o tim kiem.
- Cho ket qua: [{"action":"wait","ms":3000}].

BUOC4 - XAC NHAN:
- Neu thay danh sach video ket qua tim kiem: tra [{"action":"done","reason":"da tim nhac tren YouTube"}].
- Neu van o trang chu/chua tim thay o tim kiem: lap lai BUOC3.
- Neu bi loi/khong tai duoc: lap lai BUOC2.

QUY TAC BAT BUOC:
- Truoc moi click/type/press: dam bao cua so Chrome dang foreground. Neu bi cua so khac che hay Chrome van minimize: [{"action":"window_activate","target":"Google Chrome"}].
- Khong click o toa do x <= 10 va y <= 10.
- Chi tra ve 1 mang JSON, toi da 5 thao tac.
- TUYET DOI KHONG tóm tắt màn hình hay binh luan. Chi tra hanh dong JSON.
