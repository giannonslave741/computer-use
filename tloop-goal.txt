MUC TIEU: Moi 2 phut chup man hinh 1 lan de kiem tra Roblox con dang TRONG GAME khong.
Neu DANG TRONG GAME -> tra ve done (he thong se tu spam click giua cua so Roblox + bam T lien tuc).
Con lai -> khong spam, xu ly theo trang thai duoi day. KHONG tu dung chuong trinh, khong dua ra ket luan sau qua 1 anh.

TRANG THAI 1 - DANG TRONG GAME (thay the gioi/nhan vat/HP/Level/chat cua game, khong phai menu/homescreen Roblox):
  -> tra ve DUY NHAT: [{"action":"done","reason":"in game"}]
  (done = tin hieu "con trong game" cho he thong, KHONG dung chuong trinh.)

TRANG THAI 2 - DANG LOADING (Roblox dang load, man hinh load chua ro the gioi):
  -> [{"action":"wait","ms":3000}]

TRANG THAI 3 - DANG O MENU/HOME CUA ROBLOX (khong phai kick, khong phai dialog loi):
  UU TIEN dung muc CONTINUE de vao lai game:
  1. Tim muc "Continue" / "Bay gio" / game vua choi tren menu Roblox (thuong o tren cung, anh bia + ten game).
  2. Xac dinh game vua choi = muc Continue dau tien (ganh nhat); neu anh bia/ten game ro -> chon no.
  3. Click nut Play/Continue cua no: [{"action":"click","x":...,"y":...}] -> [{"action":"wait","ms":3000}]
  4. Khong thay muc Continue, khong ro vi tri, hoac click roi van o menu -> chay bash:
     powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Users\dongt\opencode-computer-use\tloop-relaunch.ps1"
     roi tra: [{"action":"wait","ms":3000}]
  NEU DUONG DAN HINH co ghi "KHONG chay tloop-relaunch.ps1 nua" -> CHI dung cach 1-3 (Continue), KHONG chay ps1.

TRANG THAI 4 - BI KICK / DIALOG LOI / ROBLOX LOI (co thong bao loi, thua khoi server, Roblox Error):
  - Chay bash TRUOC: powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Users\dongt\opencode-computer-use\tloop-relaunch.ps1"

TRANG THAI 5 - KHONG THAY CUA SO ROBLOX (crash/da dong):
